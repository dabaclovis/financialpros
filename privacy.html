Privacy & cookie policy

How information is handled when you browse this website or contact TrueVine Financial LLC.

Effective date: October 24, 2026.

Scope and contact

This policy describes the public website at financialpros.brotherfall.com, published for TrueVine Financial LLC. For privacy questions or requests, call 614-432-5242 or email judithdanji@yahoo.com. Insurance applications and services provided through an insurer or another platform may have separate privacy notices.

Information you choose to provide

This website has no online submission form or user account. Phone and email links open applications on your device. If you call or send an email, the receiving business and communication providers receive the information you choose to share, such as your contact details and questions. Information in an inquiry is used to respond and discuss the service you requested.

Do not include Social Security numbers, bank details, medical records, or copies of policy documents in ordinary email. Ask about a suitable secure process before providing sensitive information.

Technical information and external services

Delivering a website requires transmitting technical information such as an IP address, browser details, requested page, and request time to the hosting provider. Hosting and security providers may keep request logs under their own operational and retention practices.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's font servers, which receive technical request information. See the Google Fonts privacy explanation and Google's privacy policy. Other external links are governed by the policies of the sites you visit.

Cookies and current advertising status

The website's own code does not set cookies or use local storage, analytics trackers, or advertising scripts. Google AdSense is not currently activated in this version of the website. Your email, telephone, browser, or hosting services may have separate practices.

If Google advertising is introduced

Before advertising is activated, this policy must be updated to identify the providers actually used, and appropriate consent and privacy controls must be configured. The following explains how Google advertising cookies work; it does not mean they are currently active here.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. Some third-party advertising choices are also available through YourAdChoices.

Where required, a suitable consent mechanism must operate before relevant advertising or tracking technologies are used. Personalized Google ads for users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland require a Google-certified consent management platform under Google's applicable rules. A privacy policy alone does not obtain consent.

Sharing, retention, and requests

Communications may be handled by telephone, email, hosting, and professional service providers needed to receive or respond to your request. Do not assume that ordinary email is confidential or end-to-end encrypted. Further sharing connected with an insurance application should be explained in the relevant service and insurer notices before you proceed.

To ask what inquiry information is held, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact the business using the details above. Available rights and retention obligations depend on applicable law and the nature of the record. Identity verification may be needed; do not send identity documents through ordinary email without arranging a secure method.

Children and policy changes

The site is intended for adults researching family protection, not for children to submit personal data. If you believe a child has shared personal information, contact us. Material changes to this website's data practices should be reflected here with an updated effective date.
