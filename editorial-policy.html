Editorial standards & corrections

Useful education should explain the limits of a financial product as clearly as its potential benefits.

Who publishes this information

The website presents TrueVine Financial's services and educational resources. Our About page describes the business and founder Judith Danji. Educational articles are attributed to the business, not to an invented specialist or review board.

What the guide is, and what it is not

The family protection guide is general U.S. financial education. It is designed to help readers organize questions, compare trade-offs, and prepare for a conversation. It is not individualized insurance, investment, legal, or tax advice and has not been represented as reviewed by a licensed specialist.

AI tools assisted with drafting and organizing the educational content in this website update. AI assistance is not a professional qualification or a substitute for a qualified review. Readers should verify contract-specific statements with the insurer and obtain advice appropriate to their circumstances before making financial decisions.

Sources and examples

We prioritize links to public consumer resources from insurance regulators, government agencies, and established public institutions. Source links are provided where readers can continue their research. A link is not an endorsement by the source, and a national resource does not replace the law or policy terms that apply in a particular state.

Worked examples use explicitly hypothetical numbers and disclose important assumptions. They are not customer results, product quotes, insurer illustrations, forecasts, or coverage guarantees. We do not present invented client testimonials, credentials, or approval claims.

Currency and corrections

Insurance rules, product terms, and source pages change. This site does not promise continuous monitoring or real-time updates. Check the current source and actual contract before relying on a statement. Dates should describe a genuine publication or material update, not imply an expert review.

To report a possible error, contact us with the page address, the passage, an explanation of the issue, and a supporting primary source when available. Do not include private financial or health information in a correction request.

Commercial relationships and advertising

This is a business website that invites readers to discuss financial protection services. A free introductory conversation does not mean every product or subsequent service is free. Ask how a professional is compensated and which insurers or products they can offer.

No paid advertising or affiliate tracking is installed in the website's current code. If advertising or sponsored content is introduced, it should be clearly distinguishable from educational material and must not imply endorsement by an insurer, regulator, or Google. Privacy and consent controls must be updated before relevant tracking is activated.

Verify professional authority

Business names and job titles do not establish licensing, fiduciary status, or certification. Before a transaction, ask for license information and check it with the relevant regulator. Read the terms and disclosures for additional limits.
